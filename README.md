## The entry

`main.ts` writes one word — the word to erase from the dictionary forever — to `/output/entry.txt`, and validates it first:

```text
moist
```

## Why this word works

"Moist" is the most famously hated word in English: surveys keep crowning it the word people most want gone, and the cringe lands the instant you read it, no caption needed. The humour comes from the word itself — its damp, squelchy sound — and from the gap its deletion would leave: a whole language suddenly unable to describe a wet sponge, a cake, or a handshake. It is a real word every reader recognises, spelled correctly, with nothing added around it. Deleting it is funny at first sight because everyone already wishes it gone.

```sh
node main.ts
```
